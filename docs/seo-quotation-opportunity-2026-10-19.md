## Why this change

The selected opportunity is website-design quotation intent, rather than another broad city/service page. Authenticated Google Search Console, Web, September 20–October 17:

|---|---:|---:|---:|

The first exact quotation query maps to `/blog/website-design-cost-mahabubnagar`. All 15 of its impressions are in the United States. The second query was verified in the property-wide table; its geography was not separately captured. These are observed impressions, not keyword volumes or proof of Indian demand. India's visible query table is sparse and includes “website costs for small business” at three impressions, zero clicks and position 7.3. Query anonymization prevents a complete demand picture.

The site already has three pricing guides, two running-cost calculator placements and a comparison CSV. They were recently improved, so duplicating them or changing their titles again would not add a distinct answer. Quotation intent has a narrower gap: no dedicated itemised sample plus editable requirements brief. Current public results include quotation samples/templates, supporting that format as a relevant answer, not evidence that ranking will be easy.

Alternatives considered: another local service page has weaker visible query evidence; a recurring-cost article overlaps the existing calculator and pricing copy. The quotation resource offers the clearest additional utility and a direct route to a project brief. It is a testable opportunity, not a forecast of 50 clicks.

## Implemented

- `/blog/website-design-quotation-india`: dedicated sample, editable-brief explanation, supplier comparison, ownership/renewal/support questions and project CTA. Published prices resolve through existing tokens. No invented average prices, client outcomes or payment terms.
- `/downloads/website-design-quotation-brief.txt`: ungated editable text brief with requirements, supplier-response fields and comparison checks. No fixed prices, personal data, executable content or automatic submission.
- Contextual links from all three pricing guides. Their price-focused titles and calculators remain intact. The existing blog listing, related-post links, self-canonical, article/FAQ/breadcrumb schemas and sitemap discover the new page automatically.
