# Organic competitor research — 5 October 2026

## Decision

Prioritize the existing Mahabubnagar website-cost page, original project evidence, and links into the established service pages. Broad Hyderabad agency terms and new AI pages are longer-term work. This is a content and intent comparison, not a current Google rank report.

The target is **50 Google organic clicks during 6–20 October inclusive (15 days)**. The supplied 6 September–3 October baseline is 13 clicks / 552 impressions / 2.4% CTR / position 14.3. Fifty clicks requires about 3.3 clicks/day versus 0.46/day in that baseline: approximately seven times the daily rate. At unchanged impression pace, it would require roughly 17% CTR; at 2.4% CTR, about 2,083 impressions. These are arithmetic scenarios, not forecasts. Current evidence does not justify a promise to reach 50 within this window.

Source: [5 October site/GSC audit](seo-audit-2026-10-05.md). ## Work most likely to help within the short window

1. **Improve the page already receiving visibility.** The Mahabubnagar cost guide has 182 impressions, zero clicks and average position 12.5. Add the quote worksheet and real project context. Low CTR can reflect position and search intent as well as wording; a title edit alone is not a growth forecast. The guide changed on September 27, so log further substantive edits and avoid daily title churn.
2. **Fix evidence quality while editing.** The current ₹3,800 section speculates that cheap sites reuse designs hundreds of times, use placeholder copy and usually cover only year one. The accessible competitor evidence does not establish those claims. Replace with neutral questions about inclusions and renewals. Remove the statement that every portfolio site is live because Kings Mobile World is archived. Keep Growth Masala's actual published package terms.
3. **Publish first-hand project detail promptly.** SQC and Health Factor are real portfolio additions. Show what was built, not invented traffic lifts. Health Factor's appointment request is not a confirmed booking; SQC's lending content is client website scope, not Growth Masala financial advice. Use existing verified screenshots and link each case study into its service/pricing path.
4. **Help discovery through useful contextual links.** Ensure pricing, local services, portfolio and case studies connect naturally. Request indexing only after substantive publication and individual URL-status inspection; repeated requests do not manufacture rankings. Existing crawl checks indicate no site-wide indexing block.
5. **Protect measurement.** Count the target in Search Console Web search for October 6–20, allowing final data to settle. Track landing-page and query-cluster clicks, impressions, CTR and position separately; keep enquiries as another outcome. Referral/social visits and repeated owner searches must not be presented as earned Google clicks. At the end, compare equal non-overlapping periods and report the small sample honestly.

The next content opportunity is a recurring website-cost guide if the existing material cannot fully answer that intent. New dental-sector and AI landing pages may be valuable, but neither has validated short-window demand here. Do not dilute this sprint with many generic articles or city clones. No outreach, directory submission, paid link, browser UI action, or production change was performed in this research subtask.

## Google guidance supporting the recommendations

- Google emphasizes original information and first-hand expertise, with useful evidence rather than rewritten competitor content: [people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
- Clear, concise titles should describe the page accurately; Google can select a different title link: [title-link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link).
- Crawlable contextual links and descriptive anchor text help users and Google understand related pages: [link best practices](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable).
- Changes may take hours through months, and Google generally advises waiting weeks to assess impact: [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide). A 15-day target is therefore a stretch goal, not a promised delivery result.
- Avoid scaled low-value location content and manipulative links: [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).

## Evidence limits

The search tool also surfaced an old Growth Masala Station Road snippet despite the current audit confirming removal of that placeholder. These observations are not grounds to restore a physical-office claim. Growth Masala operates remotely from Mahabubnagar; no customer-facing premises or GBP is proposed. This research supplies no fresh search-volume estimate, exact Google rank, competitor backlink count, or independently verified competitor performance statistic.
