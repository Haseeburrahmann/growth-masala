## Technical check

- All returned HTTP 200 with a self-referencing canonical and no meta noindex directive.
- Exactly one H1 per page; unique titles and meta descriptions; all titles within the repository's 60-character target.
- Parseable JSON-LD on all pages; no images missing an alt attribute. This verifies presence and JSON syntax, not rich-result eligibility or the quality of every alt.
- robots.txt allows crawling and points to the correct sitemap.
- Fourteen descriptions exceed the repository's 160-character editorial target. This is a possible snippet-truncation issue, not an indexing failure or a Google character-limit violation. The portfolio description was shortened in this update; prioritize the others by impressions, not length alone.

## Improve clicks from existing visibility

The Mahabubnagar pricing article is the first optimization candidate, but it was already revised on September 27. Avoid rewriting it repeatedly before collecting a useful post-change window. Add genuinely useful material and local proof first; then test one clear title/snippet change and record its deployment date. Compare the same page and query cluster over non-overlapping 28-day windows, allowing for Search Console's reporting lag.

A proposed title to evaluate is “Website Cost in Mahabubnagar: Prices & Inclusions”. Do not insert an attractive starting price unless it matches the current published package and its exclusions. Google may generate different title links and snippets. Low CTR at position 12.5 can reflect limited visibility as well as wording; a new title alone is not a guaranteed fix.

Add contextual links from each new case study to the relevant service and pricing guide, with descriptive anchor text. Preserve one primary page per search intent. Keep the existing national five-page guide and Hyderabad and Mahabubnagar companions rather than creating near-identical cost articles for many towns.

Correct remaining stale proof claims in a future accuracy pass: homepage portfolio copy and the Mahabubnagar pricing article still imply every showcased project is currently live, while Kings Mobile World links to an archived case study. The portfolio metadata is now accurate and includes the Delhi project.

## Authority and measurement

- Preserve Health Factor's existing attribution. Request an optional, editorially appropriate SQC project acknowledgment only if the client agrees; no outreach was sent in this task. Do not buy links or create keyword-stuffed footer links.
- Collect two genuine client testimonials and, once available, approved outcome data. Publish measured results with dates and context rather than promising traffic or bookings.
- Confirm GA4 receives successful contact-form leads and that business-relevant events are marked as key events. Treat WhatsApp/telephone clicks as contact intent, not completed sales.
- Review Search Console weekly, but evaluate changes using longer, non-overlapping windows. Record page/query impressions, clicks, CTR, position and enquiry outcomes. No monitoring automation was created.
- Avoid a large batch of generic AI articles, copied location pages or unsupported “top agency” comparisons. Original delivered-work evidence is the stronger basis for new content.

Google's guidance supports [first-hand, useful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content), [descriptive internal links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable), and [clear, accurate title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link). Adding FAQ markup is not a click-growth strategy for an agency: [FAQ rich results are restricted mainly to authoritative government and health sites](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/08/howto-faq-changes).
