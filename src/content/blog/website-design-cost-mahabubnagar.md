## How much does it cost to run a website after launch?

The build price pays for creating the site. Keeping it available and changing it over time involves separate costs. There is no single annual figure that fits every website.

| Cost | When it is paid | How to budget for it |
|---|---|---|
| Domain registration and renewal | At registration, then at the registrar's renewal interval | Use the actual renewal quote for your chosen domain, not just an introductory offer |
| Hosting | At the provider's monthly or annual billing interval | Check the renewal rate and what the plan includes; annualize monthly charges when comparing |
| Maintenance and content edits | According to your chosen support arrangement | Growth Masala care is optional, from {{price:care-basic}} per month excluding GST |
| Paid tools or integrations | Only when your site needs them | Confirm subscriptions and usage charges separately |
| New pages or functionality | When requested | Get a separate scope and price before work starts |

**Hosting and maintenance are different services.** Hosting makes the website available online. A care plan pays for agreed work such as backups, monitoring and content changes. Check what your host already provides before choosing additional support.

Our Care Basic plan includes monthly backups, security and uptime monitoring, two content updates a month and email support. Other care plans have different scopes. Included launch support is not the same as purchasing ongoing care, and declining optional care does not change your ownership of the delivered website. Domain and hosting still need to remain active.

For a comparison before tax, use:

- **First year:** one-time build + first-year domain and hosting + care for the months selected + any separately agreed extras.
- **A later year:** domain and hosting renewals + care for the months selected + any paid tools or requested work.
- **Three years:** build once + each year's domain and hosting charges + selected care across the period + extras.

If rates stay unchanged, three years of annual renewals is three times the yearly amount. If the provider has a different introductory rate, list year one separately. Taxes must be added to the applicable charges; do not present an estimate before tax as the final invoice.

## How to compare a cheaper quote fairly

A lower price can reflect a smaller scope, an existing design or different support. It does not prove that the site is poor, and a higher price does not establish quality. Ask both suppliers to answer the same questions:

1. What pages and enquiry features will be delivered?
2. Who writes the text and supplies the photos?
3. What will the domain, hosting and any required tools cost to renew?
4. Who owns the website and the domain/hosting accounts, and what happens if you change suppliers?
5. What support is included, what counts as a revision and how are later changes charged?

![Two people at a desk reviewing a printed quote, one ticking items off with a pen](/images/blog/checking-a-website-quote.webp "Compare the written scope, recurring charges and ownership before choosing a website quote.")

Open two of each supplier's delivered sites on your own phone and connection. Check whether you can understand the services, find evidence of their work and contact the business without searching through the page. That is more informative than judging the supplier by the price alone.
