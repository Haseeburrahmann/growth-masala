# PRODUCT.md — Growth Masala

**register: brand**

Marketing surface. The design *is* the product: a visitor's impression in the first
three seconds decides whether a shop owner in Mahabubnagar believes this agency can
be trusted with ₹25,000 they have possibly already lost once before.

> Full positioning, personas, objections, competitor teardown and proof-point audit
> live in [`.agents/product-marketing.md`](.agents/product-marketing.md). This file is
> the design-facing distillation. When the two disagree, product-marketing.md wins on
> facts, this file wins on visual direction.

## Users

Owner-operators of small businesses in Mahabubnagar district, the surrounding
Telangana towns, and Hyderabad. Private schools, mobile retail and repair chains,
construction firms, lenders, shops. One person is the buyer, the champion and the
user. No marketing department, no procurement.

They browse on a mid-range Android phone, often on patchy 4G. **Mobile is the primary
surface, not the adaptation.**

The emotional starting position is not curiosity, it is suspicion: *"I've already
spent money on this once and got nothing."* Every design decision either earns
against that or spends against it.

## Brand

**Name:** Growth Masala. **Tagline:** Spice Up Your Brand Growth.
*Masala* = the blend of strategy, creativity and technology. The word is warmth and
blend, not heat and novelty. Do not turn it into chilli emoji, flame motifs, or
"spicy" orange-red. That mistake has already shipped once (see CLAUDE.md §Mistakes).

**Tone:** professional, warm, plain-spoken. Confident without hype. Anti-fluff by
design. Consultant-to-owner, never agency-to-brand. Indian English, `-ise` spellings,
₹ prices, city names stated explicitly.

**Voice words (the three that drive typography and layout):**
**Straight · Local · Built.** Not sleek, not playful, not premium. The visual
equivalent of a fixed quote handed across a table.

## Strategic principles

1. **Every visual claim must be verifiable.** The site sells "fixed quote, no
   surprises". A fabricated metric anywhere on the page contradicts the entire
   proposition. Fake counters were removed from the hero for this reason; the same
   rule retires the fabricated analytics dashboard. Show named clients and published
   prices, which are checkable, over numbers, which are not.
2. **Photography carries the locality claim.** "We are actually here" is the one
   differentiator metro competitors cannot fake. Generic SaaS illustration throws it
   away. Imagery should look like Telangana: real shopfronts, real counters, real
   light. Never Bay Area co-working stock.
3. **Published prices are a trust device, not a spec sheet.** ₹9,999 in a large
   figure does more persuasive work than a feature matrix.
4. **WhatsApp is the conversion surface.** Every path ends there.
5. **Speed is a design constraint.** A full-viewport raster in the hero pushed mobile
   LCP 2.7s → 4.9s once. Atmosphere gets drawn in CSS; rasters must earn their bytes.

## Anti-references

- **SaaS-template homepage.** Centred stack, three identical icon-title-text cards,
  gradient-text headline, fabricated dashboard mockup. The site currently carries
  traces of all four; they are being removed, not extended.
- **Metro-agency gloss.** Glassmorphism, neon, abstract 3D blobs, "we craft digital
  experiences". Reads as the Hyderabad agency the copy is arguing against.
- **Chilli / flame / spice-rack literalism.** See Brand above.
- **Vanity metrics as proof.** Impressions, reach, follower counts as headline claims.
- **Editorial-typographic lane.** Display-serif italic, drop caps, ruled columns,
  monochrome restraint. Wrong register for a trades-adjacent local service business,
  and currently the most saturated aesthetic on the internet.

## Reference lane (named, so the work does not drift into beige)

**Dark-slab agency with a hard accent block and documentary photography.**
Misiom's solid-amber capability slab against near-black; Orixo's numbered service
rows and pill-with-badge CTAs; photography of actual Indian small businesses rather
than models in an office. Navy is the room, amber is the object in it, blue is the
thing you click.

## Conversion action

Free consultation. Priority order: WhatsApp → phone → contact form → chatbot lead
capture. Portfolio view is the trust step immediately before contact.
